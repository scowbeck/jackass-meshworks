C4D · Free beta · Version 1.0

CloneSensor places clones on a surface so they never occupy the same space. Each candidate is tested first: if the space is free, the clone is kept and that space is closed. If not, the candidate is thrown away. Nothing gets pushed apart afterward.

Features

  • Tested placement

    Each clone's shape is checked against space already reserved. A fit is kept, an overlap is discarded.

  • Repeatable results

    The same seed, Max Count, settings and surface give the same clones. Every time.

  • Seven sampling modes

    Random, Poisson Disk, Blue Noise, Grid, Hex Packing, Vertex and Polygon Center.

  • Layout presets

    Crowd, Seating, Festival, Parking and Booths, in real-world sizes for your project units.

  • Collision shapes

    Sphere, Bounding Box or Footprint, tested in 3D or along the surface, sized per asset or from a shared radius.

  • Keep away

    Edges, splines and fields refuse a point before any clone is made.

  • Fill Surface

    One click fills the whole surface. Spread Across Surface reaches separate floors and regions.

  • Rules engine

    Categories, weights, priorities and clearance between groups, measured instead of guessed.

  • Flexible output

    Instances, points or cloners, with viewport overlays and full solve statistics.

Workflow benefits

  • No more hand-nudging intersecting clones before a render.
  • Crowds, seating, festival grounds, parking and booth layouts from one generator.
  • Same seed, same layout, so revisions and handoffs stay predictable.
  • Works in centimeters, meters, feet, or whatever units the project uses.
